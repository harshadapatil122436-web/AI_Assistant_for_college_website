Yes, lateral entry to second year is available for eligible vocational and ITI backgrounds, subject to seat availability.

Yes, government scholarship schemes apply per category. Check the current scholarship notice for deadlines and required forms.
Can I change branch after first year? +
Branch change requests are considered at the end of first year, subject to seat vacancy and merit.
